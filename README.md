Gamble like your life depends on it!

Play a range of card games and other mini games in this hellish bar filled with interesting and unique characters!

Lie, strategize, and bluff your way to victory! 

An upgrade is also in the works for the overall player experience.

This project was created over approximately 12 weeks by a team of 2 programmers and 7 artists.

The game can be found on its itch page: https://hamb04.itch.io/7-sins
